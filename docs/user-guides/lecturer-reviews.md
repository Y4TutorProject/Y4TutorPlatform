# Lecturer guide: reviewing tutor applications

Lecturers review tutor applications for the modules they teach. Your college administrator assigns you to each module, usually from the Moodle lecturer role.

## Access your account

1. Sign up with your college email address, using the same student sign-up page. Verify your email before signing in.
2. Sign in. Your dashboard shows your teaching modules if your email matches your imported lecturer profile.

If you don't see your modules, ask your college administrator to check that your Moodle profile uses the same email address and that you're assigned as the module's lecturer.

## Review applications

1. Open **Review tutor applications** from your dashboard, or go to `/tutor-applications`.
2. Read the student's module and grade.
3. Select **Approve** or **Reject**.

Once you approve an application, the student can tutor that module. Check the application's module and grade before you decide.
