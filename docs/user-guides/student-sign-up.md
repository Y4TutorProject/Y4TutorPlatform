# Student sign-up and courses

Use your college student email address to create an account. The platform checks that the address uses your college's student domain.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open the sign-up page and select **Student sign up**.
2. Choose your college.
3. Enter your name, username, student email address and password.
4. Select **Create account**.

The account isn't active until you verify your email.

## 2. Verify your email

1. Open the verification email sent to your student address.
2. Select the link, or open **Verify email** and follow the instructions.
3. Sign in with your username or email and password.

Verification links work once and expire after one hour. If your link has expired, request a new verification email from the sign-in page. Check your spam folder if no email arrives.

## 3. See your courses

After you sign in, your dashboard lists the courses and modules you're enrolled in. The list comes from your college's Moodle connection or from the courses your administrator added manually. If a course is missing, ask your college administrator to check your enrolment.

## 4. Apply to be a tutor

Students who have completed earlier years of their programme can apply to tutor modules from those years. First-year students have no earlier-year modules, so the **Apply to be a tutor** button doesn't appear for them.

See [Apply to be a tutor](tutor-applications.md) for the steps.
