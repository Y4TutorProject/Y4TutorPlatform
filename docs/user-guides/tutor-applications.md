# Apply to be a tutor

Students can apply to tutor modules from earlier years of their own programme. Each application covers one module and one grade.

## Before you apply

- Sign in with your verified college account.
- Check that the module is from an earlier year than your current year.
- Have your grade for that module ready. Grades are numbers from 0 to 100.

## Submit an application

1. On your student dashboard, select **Apply to be a tutor**.
2. In the application panel, select the module you want to tutor.
3. Enter your grade for that module.
4. Submit the application.

To apply for several modules, submit one application for each module. Submitting the same module again returns your existing application rather than creating a duplicate.

## Who reviews it

- **Moodle modules:** the lecturer assigned to the module reviews the application.
- **Manually created modules:** a college administrator reviews the application.

You can't review your own application.

## After approval

When an application is approved, you get tutor access for that module. If you have approved tutor access in any module, your account gets the tutor role. You keep your student role too, so you can switch between the student and tutor dashboards.

Your tutor dashboard shows your approved modules and the students who may be matched with you.
